# Rasoi Hisaab — kaise use karein 🫓

**Link kholo → apna naam chuno → PIN daalo → bas!** Phone pe login yaad rehta hai.

### Roz subah
1. **Aaj** tab kholo. Upar dikhega: *"Aaj tumhara kaam: Sabji banana 🍛"*.
2. **Kitni roti khaoge?** — +/− dabao, **Save**. (Kuch nahi daala to tumhari usual ginti maan li jaayegi.)
3. Bahar ho / nahi khaoge? **"Bahar hoon"** switch on karo. Tumhe us session mein koi kaam nahi milega.
4. Upar **roti banani** wala number = total kitni roti banani hai.

### Kaam karne ke baad
- Bada hara button **"Mera kaam ho gaya ✅"** dabao.
- Kisi aur ka kaam kiya? Neeche us kaam ka tick lagao (*"Maine bartan dhoye ✅"*) — tumhe points **+ bonus** milega.
- Roti banai? Kitni banai woh bhi daalo.
- Khaane ke baad **"Kitni khayi?"** daalo — waste ka hisaab isi se banta hai.

### ⚡ Abhi kya hua
Home pe live list: kisne kya kiya, kab. Sab ke phone pe turant dikhta hai.

### Niyam (sab ke liye same)
- Tum **sirf apna** data badal sakte ho. Doosron ka sirf dekh sakte ho.
- Kisi ki entry galat lage? **🚩 Flag** lagao — woh hamesha dikhega, koi mita nahi sakta.
- **24 ghante** baad (din khatam hone ke) entry lock ho jaati hai.
- Har badlaav **History** mein likha jaata hai — kaun, kya, pehle kya tha.

### Kaam badalna hai? 🔁
Apne kaam ke saamne 🔁 dabao → kisko dena hai chuno → woh apne phone pe **"Haan"** bolenge tabhi plan badlega.

### Plan kaise banta hai?
Formula se, sab ke phone pe same. Jiske **points kam** hain (jisne kam kaam kiya), usko pehle aur bhaari kaam milta hai. Kal jo kaam kiya, aaj wahi nahi milega. **"Kyun? 🤔"** dabao, reason dikhega.

### 📊 Hisaab
Kisne kitna kaam kiya, kaun kiska kitna kaam udhaar hai, aur roti waste ka leaderboard 🏆 (ye trophy mat jeetna 😅).

### Points badalne hain?
**Settings → Badlaav ka proposal do.** 5 mein se **3 log "Haan"** bolenge tabhi lagu hoga.

PIN bhool gaye? Flat owner se bolo. 🙏
